# Analyzing-the-Relationship-Between-CO-Emissions-and-Climate-Change-Indicators


Executed a quantitative longitudinal research study using SPSS to analyze 24 years of global environmental data (2000–2023) across five countries: the USA, China, Germany, India, and Indonesia. Built multiple linear regression and ANOVA models to evaluate how carbon emissions directly drive average temperature hikes and sea level rise, while measuring the protective, risk-reducing benefits of forest conservation and renewable energy adoption.

SPSS | Quantitative Modeling | Multiple Linear Regression | Binary Logistic Regression | ANOVA | Trend Analysis
